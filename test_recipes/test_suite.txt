TEST RECIPE SUITE — Nosh orchestrator manual/regression testing
=================================================================
Each block below is a standalone `recipeText` payload — paste one block's
recipe text (everything between the ===TEST N=== marker and the next one,
excluding the "TESTS:" comment lines) into the /process_recipe request body,
or pass it directly to generate_recipe().

For each test, check the generated commands against the "TESTS:" checklist
before that recipe. Keep this file updated as new bug classes are found —
it's the seed for the golden regression suite.


===TEST 1: Ghee / Fractional Spice / Pre-cooked Dal / Water>250ml===
TESTS:
- Ghee must NOT be dispensed via "oil dispense" (solid fat exclusion rule).
- Turmeric 1/3 tsp -> dispense count must be a whole number (round(0.333*4)=1),
  never "spice turmeric dispense 1.33 times".
- Toor dal is stated as pressure-cooked/pre-cooked -> should become a
  preparation_step, not run through raw-ingredient fallback timing.
- Water 400 ml (>250) -> must have "stove heat 8" before, dispense, then
  "stir mix_liquid 1 times", then the temperature-timeout/heat_till sequence,
  then restore prior stove heat.

**Dal Tadka**
**Ingredients:**
* Toor dal (pressure-cooked) - 200 grams
* Ghee - 2 tbsp
* Mustard seeds - 1/2 tsp
* Cumin seeds - 1/2 tsp
* Turmeric powder - 1/3 tsp
* Red chili powder - 3/4 tsp
* Salt - to taste
* Water - 400 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat ghee in the pot.
2. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds, let them splutter.
3. Add turmeric powder and red chili powder, stir well.
4. Add the pressure-cooked toor dal and mix.
5. Add salt to taste.
6. Add 400 ml of water and stir.
7. Cook for 5 minutes on low flame.
8. Turn off the stove and serve hot.


===TEST 2: AI-Assisted Cooking Override Trigger===
TESTS:
- Onion is AI-cook eligible, but a spice (turmeric/chili) is dispensed BEFORE
  the onion's "cook onion till cooked" command -> validator must flag onion
  and force a manual stir/wait replacement instead of the AI command.
- Onion (50g) shares a cooking stage with paneer (300g) -> onion's weight
  share of that stage is well under 80% -> second, independent trigger for
  the same manual-override rule.

**Onion Paneer Curry**
**Ingredients:**
* Onion - 50 grams, finely chopped
* Paneer - 300 grams, cubed
* Turmeric powder - 1/4 tsp
* Red chili powder - 1/2 tsp
* Salt - 1/2 tsp
* Oil - 30 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in the pot.
2. Add turmeric powder and red chili powder.
3. Add onion and paneer together and cook until the onion turns soft and translucent.
4. Add salt and mix well.
5. Cook for 3 minutes on medium flame.
6. Turn off the stove and serve.

**Simple Cauliflower Sabzi**
**Ingredients:**
* Cauliflower - 400 grams, cut into florets
* Cumin seeds - 1/2 tsp
* Turmeric powder - 1/4 tsp
* Salt - 1/2 tsp
* Oil - 20 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil and add cumin seeds, let them splutter.
2. Add cauliflower florets and turmeric powder, mix well.
3. Cook the cauliflower until soft and tender.
4. Add salt and mix.
5. Cook for a few more minutes on low flame.
6. Turn off the stove and serve.

**Drumstick Moringa Curry**
**Ingredients:**
* Drumstick (moringa) - 250 grams, cut into pieces
* Tamarind pulp - 30 grams
* Sambar powder - 1 tsp
* Salt - 1/2 tsp
* Water - 300 ml
* Oil - 15 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil, add sambar powder and stir briefly.
2. Add drumstick pieces and mix.
3. Add tamarind pulp and salt.
4. Add water.
5. Cook the drumstick until it turns tender and cooked through.
6. Turn off the stove and serve.

**Matar Paneer**
**Ingredients:**
* Paneer - 300 grams, cubed
* Green peas - 150 grams
* Tomato - 200 grams, chopped
* Onion - 100 grams, chopped
* Ginger garlic paste - 1 tbsp
* Turmeric powder - 1/2 tsp
* Red chili powder - 1 tsp
* Garam masala - 1/2 tsp
* Salt - 1 tsp
* Oil - 30 ml
* Water - 200 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil, add onion and cook until golden.
2. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add tomato and cook until soft, about 4 minutes.
4. Add turmeric, chili powder, and salt. Mix well.
5. Add paneer and green peas.
6. Add water and simmer for 8 minutes.
7. Add garam masala and mix.
8. Turn off the stove and serve.


===TEST 6: Explicit Total Time Header vs Vague Per-Step Wording===
TESTS:
- "Total Cooking Time: 45 minutes" is explicitly stated. Every individual
  step is deliberately vague ("for a bit", "for a while", "for some time").
  Final output's total active cook/wait time should land close to 45
  minutes because it was explicitly given — it must NOT get silently
  overridden by a RAG-calibrated figure just because the per-step wording
  is vague. (This is the exact contradiction found during the Matar Paneer
  10-min-vs-64-min test earlier — keep this as the standing regression case.)

**Chana Masala**
Total Cooking Time: 45 minutes
**Ingredients:**
* Chickpeas (pre-boiled/pressure-cooked) - 300 grams
* Onion - 100 grams, chopped
* Tomato - 150 grams, chopped
* Ginger garlic paste - 1 tbsp
* Chana masala powder - 1 tsp
* Turmeric powder - 1/2 tsp
* Red chili powder - 1 tsp
* Salt - 1 tsp
* Oil - 25 ml
* Water - 200 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil, add onion and cook for a bit until soft.
2. Add ginger garlic paste and cook briefly.
3. Add tomato and cook for a while until mushy.
4. Add turmeric, chili powder, chana masala, and salt.
5. Add the pre-boiled chickpeas and water.
6. Simmer for some time until everything comes together.
7. Turn off the stove and serve.


===TEST 7: Water Dispense Threshold Boundary (250ml vs 260ml)===
TESTS:
- Step 4 dispenses exactly 250 ml -> rule is ">250", so this must NOT get
  the "stove heat 8" + "stir mix_liquid 1 times" sequence.
- Step 6 dispenses 260 ml -> this MUST get the full heat-8/stir/heat_till
  sequence, then restore the stove heat that was active before it.

**Toor Dal Rasam**
**Ingredients:**
* Toor dal (pre-boiled) - 100 grams
* Tomato - 100 grams, chopped
* Rasam powder - 1 tsp
* Mustard seeds - 1/4 tsp
* Curry leaves - 6
* Salt - 1/2 tsp
* Oil - 10 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil, add mustard seeds and curry leaves, let them splutter.
2. Add tomato and cook until soft.
3. Add the pre-boiled toor dal and rasam powder.
4. Add 250 ml of water and stir.
5. Cook for 4 minutes on medium flame.
6. Add another 260 ml of water and mix.
7. Simmer for 5 minutes on low flame.
8. Add salt, turn off the stove, and serve.

**Rava Idli**
**Ingredients:**
* Semolina (rava/suji) - 200 grams
* Yogurt - 100 grams
* Water - 50 ml
* Mustard seeds - 1/4 tsp
* Curry leaves - 5
* Salt - 1/2 tsp
* Eno fruit salt - 1/2 tsp
* Oil - 10 ml
**Instructions:**
1. Mix semolina, yogurt, and water into a batter and let it rest for 15 minutes.
2. Heat oil, add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
3. Add this tempering to the batter along with salt and eno.
4. Pour batter into idli molds.
5. Cook suji till cooked.
6. Turn off the stove and serve hot.
